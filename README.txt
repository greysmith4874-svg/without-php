VELLUM — Free Fine Jewellery & Accessories Boutique Template (uiCookies)

Concept: A product-led single-page boutique for a fine-jewellery house, "Vellum,"
launching its autumn edition ("La Nuit d'Or"). Where a lookbook site sells a mood,
Vellum sells the pieces: a featured collection grid, a scroll-snap bestsellers rail,
a materials-and-ethics case, and a budget-based gift guide with tabbed filtering.
The feel is luxe, delicate and whitespace-heavy — a jeweller's atelier rather than
a storefront. It deliberately avoids the cream+terracotta+serif AI cliché and its
apparel-lookbook sibling by anchoring on a jewel-toned deep emerald with real
antique-gold detailing over a cool pearl neutral.

Palette:
  #143A2E  Deep emerald    (signature dark sections, hero, footer accent)
  #0E2A21  Deeper emerald  (footer, gradients)
  #F3F2EC  Cool pearl      (page background — luminous, not warm cream)
  #EAE7DD  Mist            (alternating block / image-frame fill)
  #A9843C  Antique gold    (accents, hairline rules, focus, prices)
  #C9A75E  Bright gold     (highlights on dark)
  #201F1A  Ink             (headings and body on light)

Font pairing:
  Display — Cormorant Garamond (delicate high-contrast serif; the couture face)
  Body/UI — Hanken Grotesk (clean modern grotesque; letter-spaced caps for labels)

Layout idea: numbered atelier sections (01–05) with thin gold hairline rules and
lozenge motifs; gold-framed product cards that reveal an add-to-bag button on hover;
a layered atelier split with a gold "40 steps by hand" seal; and an emerald press
band with rotating pull-quotes.

Tech: Bootstrap 5 (self-hosted vendor files, relative paths), vanilla JS only (no
jQuery), inline-SVG icons (no icon fonts), Google Fonts. IntersectionObserver
reveals, add-to-bag counter, scroll-snap rail, tabbed gift filter, press rotator and
front-end newsletter validation — all respecting prefers-reduced-motion. Mobile-first,
360px–1440px, with landmarks, skip link, :focus-visible and aria-labelled controls.
