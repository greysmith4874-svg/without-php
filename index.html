03  Materials & Ethics

Only gold and stones
we can trace.

We refuse newly-mined gold and untraceable stones — full stop. Here is exactly what goes into every Vellum piece, and the paperwork that backs it up.

Recycled 18k gold

Refined entirely from reclaimed metal by SCS-certified partners. No new mining, ever.

Traceable stones

Diamonds and gems sourced mine-to-market with named origin and certification.

Hallmarked & assayed

Every gold piece is independently assayed and struck at the assay office.

Lifetime care

Free cleaning, re-polishing and re-plating for as long as you own the piece.
